Prepared assets and source metadata are retained beside each model. Models were retrieved from the public Objaverse archive; creator metadata and current licenses were checked against Sketchfab.

The Dreamcast asset is a clean, simplified white console mesh with material colors and no texture maps, selected as the temporary free replacement for the uneven scan. Wii includes its Remote and six PBR texture maps.
Local modification: the Dreamcast front triangle has an orange plastic material. The region is recorded in orientation.json and is reapplied by prepare-models.py; the original GLB remains unchanged.

Additional local modification: curved subdivision and smooth normals on the Dreamcast shell, lid and port materials, preserving sharp crease boundaries and the orange accent. See mesh_subdivision.py and orientation.json.
